Job Scout

Your own job agent, inside a free Google Sheet. Copy the script below, then follow the steps.

On your phone? The setup happens on a laptop. Send this page to yourself, then open it there:

  1. Go to sheets.new and name the sheet Job Scout.
  2. Extensions > Apps Script. Click inside the code, press Cmd+A (or Ctrl+A), then paste with Cmd+V (or Ctrl+V) and save with Cmd+S.
  3. Do not press Run. Go back to the sheet and reload the page.
  4. In the Settings tab, pick your field, level and work style.
  5. Job Scout > Refresh jobs now. Allow access (Advanced > Go to Job Scout), then click Refresh jobs now again.
